About

I'm a Principal Product Designer who takes products from concept to production. At loanDepot, I led 0-to-1 strategy and design for the mortgage experience, turning complex lending workflows into clear, focused journeys for borrowers and loan officers.

I build working prototypes and lightweight tools with AI coding agents to get teams aligned faster, and I've managed brand systems and component libraries along the way. Before loanDepot, I designed internal tools for Microsoft Azure teams and a B2B platform for San Diego Gas & Electric.
